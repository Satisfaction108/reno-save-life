    A few safety tips

    • Drink water before, during and after exercise. Being dehydrated is hard on the kidneys.
    • Build up slowly. Extreme workouts you aren't trained for can break down muscle, and that can injure the kidneys.
    • Check with a doctor before using creatine, protein powders or pre-workout supplements.
    • Try not to rely on ibuprofen for sore muscles. It reduces blood flow to the kidneys.
