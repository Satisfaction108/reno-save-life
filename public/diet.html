What these nutrients mean
Sodium

Too much raises blood pressure, which damages the kidneys' filters over time. Adults should stay under 2,300 mg a day.

Phosphorus

Builds up in the blood when kidneys weaken. The phosphorus added to sodas and processed food is absorbed more easily than the kind in whole foods.

Potassium

Good for most people. It only becomes a problem when the kidneys can't remove the extra.

Protein

Your body needs it, but very large amounts give the kidneys more work. People with CKD are often given a daily target.
